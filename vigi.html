About Vigi

Vigi is an AI-powered SaaS platform purpose-built for the insurance industry. We help insurance companies and intermediaries improve sales performance and operational efficiency through intelligent automation.

Insurance sales and servicing involve complex products, regulatory requirements, and high volumes of customer interactions. Vigi simplifies this complexity by acting as a 24/7 AI copilot for sales teams, agents, and operations.

By combining deep insurance domain expertise with Generative AI, Vigi enables faster conversations, better customer experiences, and scalable growth — without increasing headcount.
